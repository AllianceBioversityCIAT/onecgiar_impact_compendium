**Prompt 1: Login Page – Impact Compendium Database**

**Role:** Senior Product Designer  
**Objective:** Design a clean, modern, centered login page for the Impact Compendium Database using the provided color palette and logo.

---

### Style & Branding
- Primary color: `#FFC84F`  
- Accent colors: `#F07E28`, `#D19F2A`, `#B96A28`, `#DC9700`, `#777777`  
- Background: soft gradient from `#FFF8E1` (top) to `#FFFFFF` (bottom).  
- Typography: **Inter** or **Nunito Sans**, medium weight, dark gray text.
- Logo: use `logo.svg` centered at the top of the card.

---

### Layout
- Centered vertical card layout, aligned both vertically and horizontally within the viewport.
- Card dimensions: 400px width, 420px height.
- Card background: white with subtle drop shadow (0px 2px 10px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)), rounded corners (12px).

---

### Content Hierarchy
1. **Logo section:** top center, with 32px margin from top of card.
2. **Title:** text below logo → *Impact Compendium Database* (font size 20px, color `#B96A28`).
3. **Form fields:** two stacked text fields:  
   - Username / Email  
   - Password (masked input)

4. **Primary button:** yellow (`#FFC84F`), label **Log In**, black text, bold, rounded (8px).

5. **Footer link:** small text *“Forgot password?”* below button, color `#777777`, hover underline.

### Responsiveness
- Centered card scales down to 90% width on mobile (<600px).
- Font sizes scale down to 16px for inputs and 14px for footer link.

### Notes

- Add Alliance/CGIAR branding consistency (yellow focus color, rounded UI components).

- **Position:** slides in from the **right**, overlaying up to 40% of the viewport width (max 540px on desktop).
- **Animation:** smooth 250 ms ease-in from right; shadow overlay behind.
- **Close:** top-right close icon (×) or click outside panel.

  - **Geography:** Countries and Regions listed with map-pin icon.
  - **Impact Areas:** pill tags (Nutrition, Climate, Gender, etc.) color-coded.

## 2. Global Logo Style Guidelines

### Brand Asset
Use `logo.png` as the official brand mark. The logo includes both icon and text.

### Placement Rules
| Context | Placement | Behavior |
|----------|------------|-----------|
| **Login Page** | Centered above the login card | Center alignment creates balance and strong first impression. Positioned 80 px from top of viewport. |
